  • What is Artificial Intelligence?

    Artificial Intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think, learn, and adapt.

  • How does Machine Learning relate to Artificial Intelligence?

    Machine Learning is a subset of AI that enables computers to learn patterns from data and improve over time.

  • Is Artificial Intelligence replacing human jobs?

    AI automates repetitive tasks but also creates new job opportunities in tech and related fields.

  • What are the different types of Artificial Intelligence?

    AI can be categorized as Narrow AI, General AI, and Superintelligent AI depending on its capability.
